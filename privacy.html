Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

"Cella Monastica Uploader" (the "App") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Cella Monastica channel.

Accounts

The only TikTok account ever connected to the App is the channel owner's own account (@cella_monastica). No other person's account is accessed.

Data we use

With the owner's authorization, the App uses basic account information (user.info.basic) to confirm the connected account, and the video upload/publish permissions to post the channel's own videos.

Storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine and are never published or transmitted to third parties.

No collection or sharing

The App does not collect, sell, or share personal data of any user or visitor. This website uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts.

Revocation

Authorization can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings.

Contact

Questions may be submitted via GitHub issues.
